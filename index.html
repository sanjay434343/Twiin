Cards get lost. Photos aren't safe.

Twiin is a free, open-source Android app that stores your IDs, bank cards, boarding passes and tickets as interactive 3D digital twins. It works fully offline, encrypts sensitive fields with AES-256, needs no account, and prints cards at real size. Here is what it fixes.

The problem

Physical cards get lost, damaged or left at home

One lost Aadhaar, ID or transit card means hours of queues and replacement fees.

The Twiin fix

A digital twin of every card, always with you

Scan each card once and keep an exact digital copy on your phone. Print a spare at real size whenever you need one.

The problem

Gallery photos are crooked, glary and unprotected

Anyone who opens your phone gallery can see your ID photos, and flash glare makes them hard to read.

The Twiin fix

A glare-free scanner and a locked vault

Drag four corners to straighten the card and remove glare. Cards sit behind a PIN or biometrics with AES-256 encrypted fields.

The problem

Cloud wallets want an account and your data

Many wallet apps need a sign-up and upload sensitive documents to servers you cannot see.

The Twiin fix

100% offline, zero cloud

No account, no servers, no tracking. Your cards never leave the device, and backups are an encrypted file you control.

The problem

Barcodes will not scan on a dim screen

A flat photo at low brightness fails at the ticket gate, library or store counter.

The Twiin fix

Cards that open at full brightness

Twiin raises screen brightness when you open a card, so barcodes and QR codes scan quickly. Flip to the back in a true 3D turn.

The problem

You still need a paper copy sometimes

Forms and offices ask for photocopies, and resizing a photo by hand never gives the right size.

The Twiin fix

Print at real card size on A4

Fronts and backs are laid out at 85.6 x 54 mm with cut and fold guides. Print at 100% for exact dimensions.

Questions, answered.

What is Twiin?

Twiin is a free, open-source card wallet for Android. You scan an ID, bank card, boarding pass or ticket once, and Twiin keeps it as an interactive 3D digital twin that you can tilt, flip, show at full brightness and print at real card size.

Is Twiin free, and do I need an account?

Twiin is free and open source under the MIT license. There is no account, no sign-up and no subscription.

Does Twiin work offline? Where is my data stored?

Twiin is 100% offline. Your cards are stored only on your phone. There are no servers, no cloud upload and no tracking.

How secure is Twiin?

The app is locked with a PIN or biometrics, sensitive fields are encrypted with AES-256, and backups are sealed in an encrypted file that you control.

Can Twiin replace my original ID or bank card?

No. Twiin keeps digital references to your cards. It is not a payment, NFC or government ID credential, so carry the original when a service requires it.

How do I scan a card without glare or a crooked photo?

Photograph the card, then drag four corners to straighten the perspective. Twiin also removes flash glare automatically.

Can I print my cards at the right size?

Yes. Twiin lays fronts and backs on an A4 sheet at 85.6 x 54 mm with cut and fold guides. Print at 100% (actual size) for exact dimensions.

Which devices does Twiin support, and how do I install it?

Twiin runs on Android 8 and above. Download the APK, open it, allow installs from your browser or file manager if Android asks, then add your first card. Most phones from 2017 onward use the arm64-v8a build, and the main download button works on every phone.
